# Sound effect credits / 音效鸣谢

The game bundles 42 edited audio files for 28 SFX cues and 2 ambience cues. Music is not supplied by this selection.

- UI Audio, RPG Audio and Impact Sounds by **Kenney Vleugels** (https://kenney.nl/), **CC0 1.0 Universal** (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Original pack notices and full license texts are included here.
- **SFX Kit by Wyzowl** (https://github.com/wyzowl/sfx-kit), **CC0 1.0 Universal**. The publisher describes these sounds as code-synthesised with AI assistance, not field recordings.
- **"Oculus Audio Pack 01" by Oculus VR, LLC** (https://developers.meta.com/vr/downloads/package/oculus-audio-pack-1/), licensed under **CC BY 4.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Files renamed to the UCS naming convention; OGG previews transcoded from the original WAVs. Further edited for Shanhai: excerpting, mono conversion for one-shots, level adjustment, edge fades, loop overlap and OGG/WAV conversion as individually recorded in selection.json.

版权所有者的商标不包含在声音授权中；本作与素材作者没有赞助或背书关系。

Short effects are 48 kHz, 16-bit mono WAV. Ambiences are 48 kHz stereo OGG. Edits, original paths, pinned download URLs and SHA256 are in selection.json and sources.json (runtime releases: licenses/audio; editable project: docs/free-sfx). No third-party scripts from the sound repositories are executed or included.

The authors' attribution and modification notices must accompany the Oculus-derived sounds when redistributed, including inside this game's source package. Kenney and Wyzowl credit is retained voluntarily.
